## 6. Candidate versus verified comparison and contrast (generated)

Distance is CIE76 ΔE\*ab. Under 2.3 is roughly a just-noticeable difference, and over 5 is a clearly different colour. Ratios are WCAG 2.2 and are truncated, never rounded up. "Used by a verified source" means an exact hex match.

## 7. Conclusion

### What the evidence shows

1. **One brand palette is verified in every source.** Forma, the website source, the Folio site and Forma Studio all ship the same 8 core values byte for byte: `#202421`, `#3a403c`, `#f2efe7`, `#905831`, `#47756b`, `#171a18`, `#686d68`, `#e3e0d7`. The website repository also labels them "Approved … canonical". Forma adds the dark-mode accents, status colours and marketing hover/elevated colours.
2. **None of the candidate brand colours appear in any verified source.** The only exact matches are `#ffffff`: a Forma primitive that the Echelon maps do not use, and Folio demo paper.
3. Like the verified palette, the candidate palette is charcoal plus bronze. The "Balanced Charcoal and Bronze" *direction* is consistent with the evidence. The specific hex values are not.

### Can be proposed for v1 (subject to owner approval)
