# Evidence Record

## Evidence summary

The WAI-ARIA Authoring Practices Guide documents keyboard and semantic patterns for complex widgets including sliders, multi-thumb sliders, switches, tabs, dialogs, comboboxes, grids, trees, tree grids, menus, toolbars, and splitters.

## Exact claim supported or contradicted

Complex reusable components should have explicit keyboard and accessibility contracts and should follow established APG interaction patterns where applicable.

## Source provenance

Primary W3C/WAI accessibility guidance.

## Relevant excerpt or data

APG includes patterns for nearly all of the advanced interaction primitives proposed in the component catalog.

## Interpretation

Centralizing these patterns in a design system can reduce repeated accessibility risk across applications.

## Limitations

APG examples are guidance, not a guarantee that every implementation works identically with every assistive technology.

## Counterevidence

None material.

## Reproduction or verification notes

Compare each stable advanced component against the relevant APG pattern during release verification.
