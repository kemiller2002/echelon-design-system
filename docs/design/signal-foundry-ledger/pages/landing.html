Who it is for

Three roles, one fixed meaning

Survey authors

Publish a template whose questions, branching, validation and scoring cannot change after publication.

Respondents

Answer from a link. It always holds their current answers, so they can stop and continue later.

Administrators

Import completed submissions and read results together with exactly how they were calculated.

How it works

From template to traceable result

  1. Publish a template

    Its version fixes the meaning of every answer.

  2. Respondents answer

    In the browser. An anonymous submission holds no personal information.

  3. Import and read results

    Signal recalculates deterministically and shows how every figure was derived.
